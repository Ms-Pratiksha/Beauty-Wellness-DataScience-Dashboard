# Overall Research Insight

The research indicates that the beauty and wellness industry is becoming increasingly digital and data-driven. Customers are discovering products through e-commerce, social media and AI-assisted experiences. At the same time, product ratings, reviews, ingredients, price and customer preferences provide useful information for understanding purchasing behaviour.

Therefore, data science can potentially help beauty businesses move from generic product promotion toward more personalized product discovery and recommendations.

---

# Research Gap

Although beauty platforms provide customers with a large number of products and recommendations, there remains an opportunity to combine multiple sources of information such as:

- Customer characteristics
- Skin and hair preferences
- Product characteristics
- Product ratings
- Review sentiment
- Price and budget
- Purchase history
- Customer behaviour

A data-driven approach that combines these variables could provide more relevant recommendations and generate useful marketing insights.
